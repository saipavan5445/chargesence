Technical Guide

Understanding ChargeSense History & Battery Protection

Li-ion Cycle Life Optimization
1. The 80% Safe Cutoff Rule

Charging a Lithium-ion battery to 100% holds cell voltage at a stressful 4.20V–4.35V, accelerating cathode degradation and lithium plating. Stopping at 80% reduces internal chemical strain and triples battery cycle lifespan from ~300-500 full cycles to over 1,200+ cycles.

2. CC vs CV Chemistry

From 0% to ~80%, the charger runs in Constant Current (CC) phase at maximum speed. Above 80%, the charger switches to Constant Voltage (CV) where current exponentially tapers down. The last 20% takes 40% of the total time while creating excess parasitic heat.

3. Buzzer & Email Cutoff Alert

When battery reaches target SoC, ChargeSense sounds the motherboard speaker buzzer and sends an automated email notification to your inbox. Once you unplug the charger or click "Silence Buzzer", the alarm automatically stops and the session log finalizes.
